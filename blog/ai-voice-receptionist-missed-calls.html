What does a missed call cost a small service business?

A missed call costs whatever that caller would have spent with you, because many callers ring the next business on the list instead of leaving a voicemail. The loss is largest where a first visit or job is valuable, such as clinics, law firms and home services. Your own call logs and booking rate give the real figure.

Work it out in two minutes: missed calls per week × 4.33 × the share of callers who normally book × the value of a first booking. The free missed-call calculator does the arithmetic and lets you share the result with a business partner.

How does an AI voice receptionist handle a call?

An AI voice receptionist picks up when your team cannot, greets the caller in your business name, works out what they need, answers from the information you approved, and books, reschedules or takes a message. It then sends your team a written summary, and texts any caller who hangs up within seconds.

  1. A call reaches your normal number and is forwarded to the AI when the line is busy, unanswered or closed.
  2. The AI tells the caller it is an AI assistant and asks how it can help.
  3. It answers routine questions (hours, prices you publish, location, services) from your approved knowledge base.
  4. For a new enquiry it asks your qualifying questions, then books a slot in your calendar or practice software.
  5. Urgent, sensitive or unusual calls are transferred to a person or flagged for an immediate callback.
  6. Every call ends with a summary to your team by SMS, email or CRM, and missed callers get a text-back.

How is an AI receptionist different from voicemail, a phone menu or an answering service?

An AI receptionist holds a real conversation and completes the task, while voicemail and phone menus only collect or route calls. A live answering service uses people, who usually take messages and bill per minute. The AI answers instantly, handles several calls at once and books directly, but should hand complex or emotional calls to staff.

Do callers hang up when an AI answers the phone?

Some callers prefer a person, and a badly built AI makes that worse. Callers mostly stay on when the AI says upfront that it is an assistant, keeps answers short, gets them booked quickly and offers a person whenever they ask. Review real call recordings in the first weeks and fix the questions where people drop off.

  • Disclose that the caller is speaking with an AI assistant, and say if calls are recorded.
  • Keep the greeting under ten seconds and get to the caller's reason quickly.
  • Always offer a transfer or callback from a person.
  • Track hang-ups by stage of the call and rewrite the weak points.
