How Much Revenue Does an HVAC Company Lose From Missed Calls?

The formula: missed calls per month × share of those calls that would have booked × average job value = revenue lost per month.

Worked example: 120 missed calls a month × 40% that would have booked × $450 average job = $21,600 a month, or about $259,000 a year, before counting the system replacements some of those calls would have become. The call count, booking share and $450 job value are assumptions for illustration: use your own numbers from your phone system and job history.

How Do AI Voice Agents Work for HVAC Companies?

An AI voice agent for an HVAC company answers every inbound call within two rings, identifies the service type (emergency repair, maintenance, new installation, or seasonal tune-up), books the appointment based on technician availability and geographic location, and sends a confirmation SMS — without human involvement, at any volume.

Where the platform allows it, the agent connects to field service software such as ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro or Jobber to check technician availability and schedule appointments. Emergency situations are flagged with an immediate alert to the on-call technician. All calls are transcribed and logged for quality review.

What Is HVAC Maintenance Contract Automation?

HVAC maintenance contract automation is the process of using AI to systematically contact past customers before their annual maintenance window, offer a maintenance contract, and book the service — without any human involvement.

As an illustration: if a campaign to 800 past customers, sent 60 days before peak season, signs 10–20% of them to a $200 maintenance plan, that is 80–160 plans, or $16,000–$32,000 a year in recurring revenue. Your response rate depends on your customer list and offer.

Which HVAC Markets Have the Best ROI for AI Automation?

HVAC companies in markets with long, hot seasons gain most from answering every call: the US Sun Belt (Texas, Florida, Arizona and the Southeast) and the Gulf, where air conditioning runs most of the year and many service requests arrive on WhatsApp.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI handle HVAC emergency calls?

Emergency calls are handled immediately — the AI identifies urgency through the customer's description, prioritises the call in the scheduling system, alerts the on-call technician with a full call summary via SMS, and provides the customer with an estimated response time. No call goes unacknowledged, even if all technicians are currently on jobs.

Can the AI book HVAC maintenance contracts?

Yes. A separate outbound campaign module contacts past customers 60 days before peak season, explains the benefits of a maintenance contract, handles objections about pricing and scheduling, and books the appointment directly. The campaign runs automatically — the business owner sets the criteria and reviews the results.
