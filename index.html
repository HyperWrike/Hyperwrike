How fast can a system go live?

What is an AI SDR and how does it compare to a human salesperson?

An AI SDR (Sales Development Representative) is an automated system that researches prospects, sends personalised outreach across email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp, and books qualified discovery calls to your calendar — 24/7, typically at a lower cost per meeting than hiring a human SDR.
