Short answer: DIY platforms such as Retell AI, Vapi and Bland AI charge cents per minute and give you voice infrastructure and builder tools, but you build the prompts, integrations, testing, monitoring, compliance and reporting yourself. What do DIY voice AI platforms give you?

DIY voice AI platforms such as Retell AI, Vapi, Synthflow and Bland AI give you the infrastructure to run AI phone calls: speech recognition, a language model, a synthetic voice, telephony connections and a builder dashboard or API. You pay mainly per minute of calls, and you design how the agent behaves.

  • Per-minute infrastructure: Retell and Bland bundle or itemize model, voice and transcription costs per minute; Vapi charges hosting and passes provider costs through at cost.
  • Builder tools: Retell offers a no-code dashboard or API; Vapi calls itself a developer platform with SDKs and a CLI; Bland has a dashboard builder plus SDKs.
  • Telephony: a platform number or your own carrier.

What do you still have to build yourself on a DIY platform?

On a DIY voice AI platform you still build everything that makes the agent useful to your business: the prompts and call flows, integrations with your CRM, calendar and phone system, testing, live monitoring, compliance settings and reporting. The platform documentation lists these as the customer's job, so plan for that work.

  1. Prompts and flows: write the agent prompt or a step-by-step conversation flow (Retell); define tools and structured outputs (Vapi).
  2. Integrations: connect CRM, calendar and help desk through APIs and webhooks, and attach a phone number or SIP trunk.
  3. Testing: run playground and simulation tests, including transfers and edge cases.
  4. Monitoring: review calls, analytics and alerts after launch (Bland), and roll out changes safely.
  5. Compliance: sign the right BAA or DPA, set data retention and redaction, and meet consent rules for outbound calls.
  6. Reporting: turn call data into bookings and revenue figures.

How much do Retell AI, Vapi, Synthflow and Bland AI cost?

Published voice AI platform pricing is per minute: Retell AI lists $0.07 to $0.31 a minute, Vapi charges $0.05 a minute hosting plus provider costs, and Bland AI charges $0.12 to $0.14 a minute. Synthflow publishes no price and sells annual Enterprise contracts only. Telephony, models and add-ons can raise the total.

When is a DIY voice AI platform the right choice?

A DIY voice AI platform is the right choice when you have someone technical with time to build, test and maintain the agent, your call flows are simple, and your budget is small. It is also right for developers and agencies building products, and for businesses that want to learn before committing to a larger build.

  • You have a developer or no-code builder with spare hours each week.
  • Calls are predictable: hours, prices, simple bookings, messages.
  • Integrations are standard, such as a calendar link or a CRM with webhooks.
  • Low monthly call volume, where per-minute pricing stays small.

If you only need calls answered, an off-the-shelf AI receptionist is simpler; see our dental, HVAC and law firm guides.

What is the total cost of owning a DIY voice agent?

The total cost of owning a DIY voice agent is the platform's per-minute bill plus telephony, model and add-on fees, plus your own team's time to build, test, monitor and fix it every month. We do not put a number on that time, because it depends on who builds it and how complex your calls are.

  1. Platform minutes: monthly minutes times the all-in rate, including telephony.
  2. Fixed fees: numbers, extra concurrency, plan fees and compliance add-ons.
  3. Build time: hours to write prompts, connect systems and test.
  4. Running time: hours each week to review calls, fix answers and update prices or hours.
  5. Risk: missed bookings or wrong answers while it is tuned.

Frequently asked questions

Should I build an AI voice agent myself or buy one?

Build on a platform if you have technical time and simple calls; buy an off-the-shelf AI receptionist if you only need calls answered; hire an agency only if missed calls cost you significant revenue.

Synthflow vs Vapi vs Retell: which voice AI platform should I use?

Retell suits teams that want a no-code dashboard with an API option; Vapi suits developers who want to pick each provider; Synthflow now sells annual Enterprise contracts with vendor launch support.

Retell vs Vapi: what is the difference?

Retell lists $0.07 to $0.31 a minute all-in and offers a no-code builder. Vapi charges $0.05 a minute hosting plus provider costs, and targets developers. With both, you build the agent.

Is managed voice AI cheaper than a custom build on Vapi or Retell?

Usually not in cash terms. A managed service only costs less overall if your own build and maintenance time is expensive or scarce.

What do I need to configure when setting up an AI receptionist?
