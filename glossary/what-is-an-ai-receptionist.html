What is an AI receptionist?

By the Hyperwrike team · Last reviewed 13 November 2026

Book a free 20-minute audit
Short answer: An AI receptionist is software that answers a business's phone calls in natural conversation, 24 hours a day. It answers questions, books appointments into your calendar, takes messages and passes urgent or complex calls to staff. It replaces voicemail and phone menus rather than replacing people.

How does an AI receptionist work?

An AI receptionist connects to your phone line, converts the caller's speech to text, uses a language model plus your business information to decide what to say, and replies with a synthetic voice. When the caller wants to book, it checks your calendar or practice software and confirms a slot.

  1. A call arrives on your number, or is forwarded when your line is busy, unanswered or closed.
  2. Speech recognition turns the caller's words into text.
  3. The AI follows rules and knowledge you set: services, prices, hours, booking policy, escalation rules.
  4. It books, reschedules, answers or takes a message, and sends a summary to your team by SMS, email or CRM.
  5. Anything sensitive or unusual is transferred to a person or flagged for a callback.

What can an AI receptionist do, and what can it not do?

An AI receptionist handles predictable calls well: bookings, reschedules, opening hours, pricing questions, directions and lead qualification. It is weaker at complaints, emotional conversations, complex negotiations and anything outside the information you gave it. Good setups escalate those calls to a person with a written summary.

Frequently asked questions

Is an AI receptionist the same as an answering service?

No. An answering service uses human agents, usually taking messages and billing by the minute. An AI receptionist is software that answers instantly, handles many calls at once and can book directly into your calendar.

Do callers know they are speaking to AI?

They should. Tell callers they are speaking with an AI assistant and whether calls are recorded; in many places this is also a legal expectation.

Which businesses use AI receptionists?

Businesses where a missed call is a lost booking: dental practices, med spas, clinics, HVAC and plumbing companies, roofers, law firms, real estate agents and mortgage brokers.

Can an AI receptionist book into my existing software?

Usually, if your booking or practice-management software has an API or calendar integration. Check this before buying, because it decides whether the AI books directly or only takes messages.
