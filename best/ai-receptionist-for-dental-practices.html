How did we evaluate AI receptionists for dental practices?

We compared dental AI receptionists on six points taken from each vendor's own website: published pricing, practice-management integrations, 24/7 coverage, booking on the call, HIPAA and BAA claims, and the size of practice each one suits. We did not run test calls, so treat this guide as a shortlist, not a verdict.

  • Published pricing: the vendor's own pricing page, or "not published".
  • Integrations: which practice-management systems (PMS) the vendor names.
  • 24/7 coverage: around the clock, or after hours only.
  • Booking: books on the call, texts a link, or takes a message.
  • Compliance claims: what the vendor states about HIPAA, BAAs or SOC 2. We report claims; we did not audit them.
  • Who it fits: business size, existing software and call volume.

Sources are each vendor's own pages, checked 13 November 2026. We did not test call quality. Hyperwrike is our own service, listed as the done-for-you option.

Which dental AI receptionist fits which practice?

The right dental AI receptionist depends on your practice software and budget. Weave customers should start with Weave's add-on, budget-conscious practices on Dentrix or Open Dental should look at My AI Front Desk, groups wanting tiered plans should look at Viva AI, and practices that want a person on every call should consider Ruby.

Arini

Best for: dental practices and DSOs that want a dentistry-only AI receptionist with patient reactivation.

Arini answers inbound calls, reactivates patients and offers online scheduling, and states that its agents work around the clock. Limitation: pricing is not published (demo only), and we found no named PMS integrations or HIPAA/SOC 2 claim on its pages.

Viva AI

Best for: dental offices and DSOs that want published tiered pricing and a choice of after-hours-only or full automation.

Viva AI answers calls, texts, webchat and email 24/7 in 100+ languages and states it is SOC 2 Type II certified and HIPAA compliant with a BAA. Plans run from $349 a month (Gold) to $1,199 (Diamond). Limitation: billing is credit-based (10 credits per AI call minute, $0.08–$0.10 per credit overage), and Gold covers after hours only.

Weave AI Receptionist

Best for: practices already on Weave's phone and communications platform.

Weave answers calls and texts day or night, hands off to staff when needed, and states it is designed to meet HIPAA standards. Limitation: the add-on price is not published (Base Plan from $199/mo), and automatic booking needs a qualified PMS.

My AI Front Desk

Best for: smaller practices wanting a low-cost AI receptionist with named dental PMS integrations.

The dental page names Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, Curve and Practice-Web, states HIPAA + BAA on every plan, and offers 24/7 answering with in-call booking and a 7-day free trial. Limitation: the pricing page shows $99 a month for 200 voice minutes ($79 billed annually), while the dental page says "Flat $109/month"; extra usage is billed in credits.

Smith.ai

Best for: practices that want AI answering with the option of live North America-based agents.

Smith.ai offers AI-first, hybrid and human-first answering, with live agents staffed 24/7 and no overseas agents. Its free AI plan includes 24/7 answering and scheduling. Limitation: billing is per call with overage, and we found no HIPAA claim or dental PMS integration on its pricing or dental pages.

Ruby

Best for: practices that want every call answered by a live person.

Ruby uses live receptionists 24/7, including weekends and holidays, with AI only behind the scenes. It states HIPAA-compliant services and offers Spanish and bilingual handling. Limitation: billing is per minute with no stated overage rate, and no dental PMS booking integration is named.

Hyperwrike

Best for: multi-chair practices and groups losing significant revenue to missed calls, slow follow-up and no-shows.

Hyperwrike builds and runs answering, booking, reminders, recall and weekly reporting for you. Limitation: it costs $8,000 to $20,000 a month, far more than any tool above.

How should a dental practice test an AI receptionist before buying?

A dental practice should test an AI receptionist with its own calls before signing. Confirm the PMS booking works live, get any BAA in writing, check how emergencies and clinical questions are escalated, and compare the billing unit, whether minutes, credits or calls, against a normal week of call volume.

  1. Count missed and after-hours calls in one normal week.
  2. Watch a demo booking into your own PMS.
  3. Get the BAA and data terms in writing.
  4. Call with a dental emergency and a clinical question; check it escalates.
  5. Model the bill at your volume, including overage.
  6. Read every transcript for the first two weeks.

Frequently asked questions

Will an AI receptionist vendor sign a BAA, and is HIPAA included or an add-on?

It varies. Viva AI states HIPAA with a BAA, and the My AI Front Desk dental page states HIPAA + BAA on every plan. We found no claim from Arini or Smith.ai. Get a signed BAA before go-live.

Is an AI dental receptionist worth it for a small practice?

Often, if you miss calls you cannot return quickly. Start with a low-cost tool, count booked appointments for a month, and compare that with the fee.

Can an AI receptionist book appointments directly into Dentrix, Open Dental or Eaglesoft?

Some can. My AI Front Desk names Dentrix, Eaglesoft and Open Dental on its dental page, and Weave books automatically only for qualified PMS. Ask every vendor to book into your own system during the demo.

What are the risks of using an AI dental receptionist?

Wrong answers, poor handling of emergencies or clinical questions, booking errors and weak data terms. Reduce them with a written script, escalation to staff, a signed BAA and weekly transcript reviews.

What features and support should a dental practice look for?

Look for direct PMS booking, cover that matches your gaps, a BAA, escalation to staff, reminders, clear billing and a named person to fix problems. Basics: what an AI receptionist is.
