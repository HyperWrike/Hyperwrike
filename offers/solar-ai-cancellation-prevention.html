You're Losing 1 in 5 Signed Solar Deals Before They Ever Install

Solar installers typically lose 15-30% of signed contracts to cancellation during the 30-90 day gap between signing and installation. HyperWrike's AI agent stays engaged with every customer during this window and flags at-risk accounts before they cancel.

The Problem

The sale isn't done when the contract is signed — it's done when the system is installed. Between those two points, buyer's remorse, financing anxiety, and competitor re-pitches cause 15-30% of signed deals to fall apart. Your sales reps have already moved on to the next deal by the time this happens.

How We Solve It

HyperWrike builds an AI nurture agent that stays in contact with every customer through the entire install-wait period — answering financing questions, reinforcing their decision, and checking in regularly. If a customer shows any sign of wavering, the system flags the account to a human closer immediately, before it becomes a cancellation. After installation, an automated review and referral sequence turns happy customers into new leads.

What You Get

  • A full nurture sequence covering the entire install-wait window
  • Automated risk-flagging and human escalation
  • Post-install review and referral automation
  • Monthly cancellation-rate reporting

Why This Pays for Itself

At an average install value of $20,000-$30,000 with 20-25% margins, saving just one cancellation per month protects $4,000-$6,000 — saving two or three easily clears $10,000-$18,000.

Who This Is For

Residential solar installers with a 30-90 day contract-to-install gap and consistent monthly sales volume.
