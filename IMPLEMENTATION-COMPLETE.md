## 🔍 SEO/AEO/GEO OPTIMIZATION STACK

### Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)
**Target AI Systems:**
- ChatGPT Search (OpenAI)
- Perplexity AI
- Claude (Anthropic)
- Google Gemini
- Google AI Overviews

**AEO Implementation:**
- ✅ Direct answer blocks at top of every page (numbers-first format)
- ✅ Quotable standalone statements for AI extraction
- ✅ Question-based H2 headings matching natural language queries
- ✅ FAQ sections with natural language Q&A pairs
- ✅ Problem-solution frameworks with specific ROI metrics
- ✅ Industry-specific FAQs (60+ questions across all offers)

### Structured Data (Schema Markup)
**Schemas Implemented:**
1. **Organization Schema** (Homepage)
   - Company info, aggregate rating (4.9/5 from 127 reviews)
   - Service areas: 6 countries explicitly listed
   
2. **Service Schema** (20 offer pages)
   - Each offer as distinct service with pricing, area served
   - Multi-country coverage in every service schema
   
3. **FAQPage Schema** (43 pages)
   - 20 offer pages with industry-specific FAQs
   - 23 location pages with local market FAQs
   - Global FAQs on all pages
   
4. **LocalBusiness Schema** (23 location pages)
   - City-specific business listings
   - Local phone, timezone, currency indicators
   
5. **Article Schema** (5 blog pages)
   - Author, publish date, article structure
   
6. **BreadcrumbList Schema** (All pages)
   - Navigation hierarchy for all pages

### AI Crawler Optimization
**llms.txt File** (`/public/llms.txt`)
- 2,000+ word comprehensive content document
- Covers: all services, industries, pricing, results, technical specs
- Formatted for extraction by AI crawlers
- Target systems: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Google AI

### International SEO Structure
**Multi-Country Support:**
- Area served markup: US, UK, AU, AE, IN, DE
- Multi-currency: USD, GBP, AUD, AED, INR, EUR
- Channel preferences: SMS (US/UK/AU), WhatsApp (UAE/India/Europe)
- Compliance frameworks: TCPA, UK GDPR, Privacy Act 1988, UAE law, DPDP Act
- Timezone indicators: Local time zones per location
- Language: English (with regional spelling variations ready)

### Traditional SEO
**On Every Page:**
- Unique `<title>` tags under 60 characters
- Unique meta descriptions under 155 characters
- Single H1 with primary keyword
- H2/H3 structure with semantic keywords
- Alt text on all images
- Keyword-rich URL slugs

- Mobile-responsive design
