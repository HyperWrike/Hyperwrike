Your CRM Is Sitting on Deals You've Already Paid to Acquire

Real estate teams typically have hundreds or thousands of past clients and leads in their CRM that never get worked because agents are focused on new business. Hyperwrike's AI agent runs ongoing reactivation and referral campaigns across the full database.

The Problem

Every contact in your CRM cost you something — ad spend, time, a referral fee, a closed deal. Most of them are never touched again. Your agents are busy chasing new leads, not mining the database you already have. That's revenue sitting untouched.

How We Solve It

Hyperwrike builds an AI outreach agent that runs ongoing campaigns across your entire database — market updates, "thinking of selling?" check-ins, and direct referral asks. Every closing automatically triggers a referral request sequence. Warm responses are qualified by the agent and routed directly to your team to close.

What You Get

  • Full database segmentation and campaign setup
  • Ongoing monthly AI outreach via email and SMS
  • Automated referral sequence triggered at every closing
  • Monthly reporting: contacts engaged, warm leads surfaced, deals attributed

Why This Pays for Itself

At an average commission of $8,000-$15,000, a single reactivated seller or referral covers the entire monthly cost — most teams see 2-3 per month once the system is running.

Who This Is For

Real estate teams or brokerages with 500+ contacts in their CRM and 3 or more agents.
