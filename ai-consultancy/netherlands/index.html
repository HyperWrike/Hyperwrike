Does the EU AI Act Apply to an AI Receptionist or Chatbot?

Yes, through its transparency rules, which came into effect in August 2026: people must be made aware that they are interacting with an AI system, such as a chatbot or voice agent, unless it is obvious. Most customer-service and booking systems are not high-risk under the Act.

Our agents say they are AI at the start of every conversation and offer a route to a person. Uses that the Act treats as high-risk, such as AI that screens job candidates or decides access to credit, carry much heavier obligations. We flag them in discovery rather than building them by default.

Reference: European Commission: AI Act. General information, not legal advice.

How Does GDPR Work With a Consultancy Based in India?

Personal data accessed from outside the EU needs a lawful transfer mechanism, most often the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, plus a data processing agreement. Hyperwrike sets these up with you during discovery and keeps data hosted where you choose.
  • Data processing agreement covering what the AI collects, why, and for how long.
  • Standard contractual clauses for any access to personal data from India.
  • Hosting and retention agreed up front, with call recordings and transcripts kept only as long as you need them.

References: European Commission: standard contractual clauses · Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Is AI Telemarketing Allowed in the Netherlands?

Since July 2021, businesses may only make telemarketing calls to consumers who have given prior consent or are existing customers. Marketing emails and texts also need consent, apart from the exception for existing customers. So our Dutch systems focus on inbound enquiries, documents and back-office work, and follow-up with people who asked to be contacted, not cold outbound calling.
