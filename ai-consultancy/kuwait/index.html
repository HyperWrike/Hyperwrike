Why Do Kuwaiti Businesses Need WhatsApp-First AI?

In Kuwait, as across the Gulf, most customers would rather send a WhatsApp message than fill in a form or wait on hold, and many message several businesses at once. Enquiries peak in the evening and over the Friday–Saturday weekend, when teams are smallest. An AI agent on the official WhatsApp Business Platform replies in seconds, asks the questions your team would ask, books the appointment or viewing, and hands the hot leads to your staff with the full conversation attached.

Can the AI Reply in Arabic?

Arabic flows can be built with current language models. Customers often switch between Arabic and English in the same chat, so we scope Arabic per project, including the tone and dialect your customers expect. We test it with your team before go-live. English is the default, and the agent hands over to a person whenever it is unsure.

Which Rules Apply to AI Messaging in Kuwait?

  • WhatsApp: outbound business messages use approved templates and only go to people who have opted in, under WhatsApp's business messaging policy.
  • Data privacy: Kuwait's Communication and Information Technology Regulatory Authority (CITRA) has issued data privacy rules. We map what personal data the system collects, where it is stored and who can access it during discovery, and check which rules apply to your sector.
  • Health data: clinic systems are scoped with extra care around patient information and where it is hosted.

References: CITRA · WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy. General information, not legal advice.
