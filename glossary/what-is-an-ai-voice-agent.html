What is an AI voice agent?

By the Hyperwrike team · Last reviewed 13 November 2026

Book a free 20-minute audit
Short answer: An AI voice agent is software that holds spoken phone conversations on a business's behalf, both inbound and outbound. It can answer calls, qualify leads, book appointments, call new enquiries back within a minute, send reminders and reactivate past customers. An AI receptionist is one type of AI voice agent.

What is the difference between an AI voice agent and an AI receptionist?

An AI receptionist is an AI voice agent focused on inbound calls: answering, booking and taking messages. "AI voice agent" is the wider term and includes outbound work such as calling new leads within 60 seconds, appointment reminders, quote follow-up and calling past customers to rebook. Many businesses use both.

What rules apply to outbound AI voice calls?

Outbound AI calls are regulated. In the US, the FCC ruled in 2024 that AI-generated voices count as "artificial" voices under the TCPA, so marketing calls need prior express consent. In the UK, PECR requires specific consent for automated marketing calls. In the UAE, telemarketing rules restrict calling hours and require registered numbers.

Sources: FCC declaratory ruling on AI voices (FCC 24-17), ICO guide to PECR. General information, not legal advice.

Frequently asked questions

Do AI voice agents sound human?

Modern AI voices sound natural and can handle interruptions, but callers should still be told they are speaking with an AI assistant.

Can AI voice agents speak other languages?

Yes. Most current speech models support many languages; quality varies by language and accent, so test with real callers before going live.

Do I need to build an AI voice agent myself?
