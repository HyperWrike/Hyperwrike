What is an AI SDR?

An AI SDR is a system that does the repetitive work of a sales development representative: finding companies that match your ideal customer profile, researching them, writing personalised outreach, following up and booking meetings. It is not a person and should not pretend to be one; your salespeople still run the discovery calls and close deals.

It sits alongside two other systems Hyperwrike builds: speed-to-lead automation for inbound enquiries and database reactivation for old leads already in your CRM.

How does an AI SDR system work?

An AI SDR system works as a pipeline. It builds a list from sources such as LinkedIn, Apollo, Google Maps and industry directories, researches each account, drafts a short personalised message, sends a multi-step sequence across email and LinkedIn, handles replies, and books interested prospects straight into a salesperson's calendar with notes.

  1. Define the ideal customer profile: industry, size, location, job titles and buying signals.
  2. Build and verify the list, removing bad addresses and anyone who has opted out before.
  3. Research each account and write a short message that references something specific and true.
  4. Send a follow-up sequence across email, LinkedIn and, where allowed, phone.
  5. Classify replies: interested, not now, wrong person, opt-out. Opt-outs are suppressed immediately.
  6. Book interested prospects into the calendar and log everything in your CRM.

AI SDR vs human SDR: which is better?

Neither wins on everything. An AI SDR is better at volume, consistency and speed: it researches and follows up every day without gaps. A human SDR is better at judgement, live conversations and complex accounts. Most B2B teams get the best result when AI does research and follow-up, and people handle conversations.

Is AI cold outreach legal in the US, UK and UAE?

AI cold outreach is legal when it follows each market's rules. US commercial email must meet CAN-SPAM, which has no business-to-business exception. UK companies can usually be emailed without consent, but sole traders and many partnerships cannot. AI-voice cold calls carry extra rules in the US and UAE. Check with counsel first.

Official guidance: FTC CAN-SPAM compliance guide; ICO guidance on business-to-business marketing; FCC declaratory ruling on AI-generated voices (FCC 24-17). UAE telemarketing rules set calling hours and registration requirements; see AI voice agents in Dubai and the UAE and our UK page for market notes. General information, not legal advice. Check with your own counsel or compliance adviser.

How do you protect email deliverability with AI outreach?

You protect deliverability by sending less, not more. Use separate sending domains, warm them up slowly, verify every address, keep daily volumes per inbox low, write plain messages without heavy links, and honour every opt-out at once. An AI SDR that blasts thousands of identical emails damages your domain and your brand.

  • Separate outreach domains so your main domain stays protected.
  • Authenticate domains (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) before the first send.
  • Verify addresses and remove role accounts and bounces.
  • Cap daily sends per inbox and spread them across the working day.
  • Review a sample of AI-written messages every week for accuracy and tone.

Who is an AI SDR system right for?

An AI SDR system suits B2B agencies, SaaS companies, consultancies and service firms with a clear ideal customer profile, a deal size large enough to justify outbound, and salespeople with time to take more meetings. It is a poor fit if you cannot yet describe who buys from you or why they buy.

Frequently asked questions

Will AI outreach damage our brand?

Only if it is careless. Short, accurate, relevant messages at modest volume protect your brand; mass generic emails harm it. Review samples weekly and stop any sequence that draws complaints.

Does an AI SDR replace our sales team?

No. It replaces manual list building, research and follow-up. Your salespeople take the booked meetings, run discovery and close.

Which CRMs can it work with?

Most CRMs with an API, such as HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive or Zoho. The integration is confirmed during the free audit.

Who owns the lists, domains and sequences?

You do. The client owns the system, the sending domains, the data and the message library, and receives weekly reporting on sends, replies and meetings booked.

Can it also reactivate our old leads?

Yes, with valid consent where required. A database reactivation campaign often finds warm conversations faster than cold outreach.
