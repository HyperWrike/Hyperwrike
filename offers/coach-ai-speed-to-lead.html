Why do webinar and VSL leads go cold so fast?

Webinar and VSL leads go cold fast because interest peaks at the moment someone opts in. Within hours they are back in their inbox, their feed and other offers. Research published in Harvard Business Review found most companies do not respond to online leads nearly fast enough, and coaching funnels are no exception.

Source: Harvard Business Review, The Short Life of Online Sales Leads. See current sourced figures in our speed-to-lead benchmarks and the definition of speed-to-lead.

How does an AI speed-to-lead system work for coaches?

An AI speed-to-lead system texts or calls every new opt-in within 60 seconds, asks a few qualifying questions about goals, budget and timing, and books a sales call straight into your calendar. Hot leads trigger a real-time alert to your setter or closer, and everyone else goes into a follow-up sequence.

  1. Opt-in arrives from your webinar, VSL, quiz or lead form.
  2. The AI texts or calls within 60 seconds, introducing itself as an assistant.
  3. It asks your qualifying questions and checks fit for your programme.
  4. Qualified leads pick a sales-call slot; hot leads alert your closer immediately.
  5. Unqualified leads get your nurture content instead of a sales call.
  6. Monthly reporting covers response time, booked calls, show rate and close rate.

This is the same AI voice agent approach we use for mortgage brokers and real estate teams.

How do you reduce no-shows on booked sales calls?

You reduce sales-call no-shows with a short confirmation sequence and an easy way to reschedule. Send a confirmation at booking, a reminder the day before, another on the morning of the call and a final nudge shortly before it starts. Anyone who misses the call gets an instant rebooking offer instead of being forgotten.

Is it legal to text and call opt-ins with AI?

It can be, with the right consent. In the US, AI-generated voices count as artificial voices under the TCPA, so marketing calls and texts generally need prior express consent, which your opt-in form should capture clearly. In the UK, PECR sets consent rules for marketing texts and calls. Check your forms with counsel.

Official guidance: FCC declaratory ruling on AI-generated voices (FCC 24-17); ICO guide to PECR. Keep a record of when and how each lead gave consent, and honour opt-outs immediately. General information, not legal advice. Check with your own counsel or compliance adviser.
