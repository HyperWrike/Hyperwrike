What Is the Revenue Impact of Missed Calls for a Dental Practice?

Missed new-patient calls × share that would have booked × value of a new patient = revenue lost. Worked example: a practice missing 60 new-patient calls a month, where 30% would have booked and a new patient is worth $2,000 in their first year, loses about $36,000 a month, before the longer-term value of those patients. The call count, booking share and $2,000 value are assumptions for illustration.

Work out your own number: missed new-patient calls per month × share that would have booked × value of a new patient = revenue lost per month. Your phone system shows the missed calls; your practice software shows what a new patient is worth to you.

How Does an AI Receptionist Work for a Dental Practice?

An AI dental receptionist answers every inbound call within two rings, identifies whether the caller is a new or existing patient, answers treatment and pricing questions from the practice's knowledge base, and books appointments directly into the practice management system — 24 hours a day, including evenings and weekends.

Where the system allows it, the AI connects to dental practice management software such as Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Carestream and Exact to check availability. Emergency dental enquiries — toothache, broken crown, lost filling — are triaged by urgency and routed to emergency slots or on-call staff with a full call summary.

What Is Dental Patient Recall Automation?

Dental patient recall automation is the use of AI to systematically contact every patient due for a 6-month check-up or hygiene appointment, deliver a personalised message referencing their last visit and the treatment due, and book the appointment directly — without front desk involvement.

A practice with 2,000 active patients on six-monthly recall has about 330 patients due every month (2,000 × 2 ÷ 12). AI automation ensures every patient in the recall window is contacted, not just those the front desk remembers to call.

How Does Dental No-Show Prevention Work?

Dental no-show prevention AI sends four automated reminders before each appointment — at 7 days, 48 hours, 24 hours, and 2 hours before — across SMS, voice call, and WhatsApp. In a study of 1.6 million appointments across 64 dental practices over five years, automated reminders cut no-shows by about 23% (Sesame Communications via Dental Tribune).

When a patient reschedules through the reminder sequence, the AI immediately offers the released slot to patients on the waitlist. The net effect is higher chair utilisation without extra front desk work.
