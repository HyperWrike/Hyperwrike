Which Privacy Laws Apply to AI Systems in Canada?

PIPEDA covers most private-sector businesses. Alberta, British Columbia and Quebec have their own private-sector privacy laws, and Quebec's Law 25 adds duties such as a privacy impact assessment before personal information is sent outside Quebec.

An AI system creates call recordings, transcripts and CRM records, all of which are personal information. During discovery we map what is collected, where it is stored and processed, who can access it and how long it is kept, so you can explain it in your privacy notice and, in Quebec, complete the assessments Law 25 requires.

References: Office of the Privacy Commissioner: PIPEDA · Summary of privacy laws in Canada · Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec. General information, not legal advice.

Do AI Texts and Calls in Canada Need Consent?

Commercial texts and emails fall under CASL, which requires consent, clear sender identification and an unsubscribe option. Sales calls must respect the National Do Not Call List and the CRTC's telemarketing rules, and automated sales calls need the person's express consent.

That is why our Canadian systems lead with answering calls customers make to you and following up with people who asked to be contacted. Every follow-up text identifies your business and includes an opt-out, and outbound campaigns only run on records with valid consent.

References: Canada's anti-spam legislation · National Do Not Call List. General information, not legal advice.

Can the AI Work in French for Quebec Customers?

Customers in Quebec have the right to be served in French. Current speech and language models support French, so a voice or text agent can answer in French, or ask callers which language they prefer. We scope French per project and test it with your team before go-live, just as we do for Spanish and Arabic in other markets.
