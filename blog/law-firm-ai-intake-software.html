Why do law firms lose potential clients after hours?

Law firms lose potential clients after hours because legal problems do not keep office hours. Someone arrested, injured or served with papers in the evening wants to talk to a lawyer now, and if a firm's phone goes to voicemail they often call the next firm in the search results. The first firm to respond usually books the consultation.

Personal injury, immigration, criminal defence and family law firms feel this most, because enquiries are urgent and several firms are contacted at once. Count your own after-hours calls and web forms for one month, then put a value on them with the missed-call calculator.

What does AI intake software do for a law firm?

AI intake software answers calls and web enquiries 24/7, asks the screening questions your attorneys approve, collects names for a conflict check, books the consultation with the right attorney and sends a written summary. Leads who do not book get a polite follow-up sequence by SMS and email until they book or opt out.

  1. Enquiry arrives by phone, web form, chat or WhatsApp.
  2. The AI identifies itself as an assistant and says it cannot give legal advice.
  3. It asks your screening questions: matter type, dates, location, opposing party names and urgency.
  4. Opposing party names go to your staff for a conflict check before booking is confirmed.
  5. It books the consultation in the correct attorney's calendar, with no double-booking.
  6. Attorneys receive an intake summary; documents the client uploads can be summarised for attorney review, never sent as advice.

Is it ethical for a law firm to use AI for client intake?

Yes, it can be, provided the firm keeps meeting its existing duties. The ABA's Formal Opinion 512 says lawyers using generative AI must still meet duties of competence, confidentiality, communication and supervision. Information from prospective clients is protected under Model Rule 1.18. Your state bar or regulator has the final word, so check with ethics counsel.

Sources: ABA Formal Opinion 512 on generative AI tools; ABA Model Rule 1.18 (duties to prospective clients). In England and Wales, solicitors should check the SRA Code of Conduct for Solicitors. Attorney advertising and solicitation rules vary by state and also apply to outbound follow-up. General information, not legal advice. Check with your own counsel or compliance adviser.

Can AI intake cross into the unauthorized practice of law?

AI intake can cross the line if it tells callers whether they have a case, what it is worth or what they should do. A safe design collects facts, explains your consultation process and books the appointment, while every question about merits, deadlines or strategy goes to a lawyer. Your attorneys should approve the full script.

Is a legal answering service or an AI receptionist better for a law firm?

An AI receptionist suits firms that want every enquiry screened and booked instantly at any hour. A legal answering service suits firms that want a person on every call and mainly need messages taken. Many firms combine both: AI handles first response and booking, while people take sensitive calls and existing clients.

Frequently asked questions

Does AI intake create an attorney-client relationship?

It should not. Scripts should state that the caller is speaking with an assistant, that no legal advice is given and that no relationship exists until the firm confirms it. Ask ethics counsel to approve the wording.

Can the AI run conflict checks?

It collects the names and details a conflict check needs and passes them to your staff or practice-management system. A person should clear conflicts before the consultation is confirmed.

Does it work for UK solicitors?

Yes. The same intake design works for UK firms, with UK GDPR and SRA conduct rules in mind. See AI automation for UK businesses.

Which practice areas suit AI intake best?

High-volume, time-sensitive areas such as personal injury, immigration, family and criminal defence, plus conveyancing firms answering routine progress questions.
