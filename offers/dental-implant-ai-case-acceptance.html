Your Implant Cases Are Stalling Because Follow-Up Stops Too Soon

Dental implant cases worth $3,000-$15,000 require multiple follow-up touches over several weeks to convert, but most practices give up after one or two attempts. Hyperwrike's AI agent runs a structured multi-week nurture sequence that recovers 2 or more additional accepted cases per month.

The Problem

An implant case is a major financial decision for most patients — it takes time, reassurance, and clarity around financing. Your team follows up once, maybe twice, then the treatment plan sits untouched while the patient quietly decides not to move forward.

How We Solve It

Hyperwrike builds an AI nurture agent that follows every open treatment plan with a structured, multi-week sequence — answering financing questions (CareCredit, Sunbit, etc.), sharing relevant testimonials, and automatically re-booking consults for anyone who's gone quiet. Every open case is tracked until it's accepted or clearly declined.

What You Get

  • A nurture sequence built around your specific case-acceptance funnel
  • Financing objection-handling built into the agent
  • Automated consult re-booking
  • Monthly case-acceptance reporting

Why This Pays for Itself

At an average case value of $6,000, converting just 2 additional cases per month generates $12,000 in recovered revenue.

Who This Is For

Cosmetic and implant dental practices doing $1M+ in annual revenue with in-house or third-party financing options.
