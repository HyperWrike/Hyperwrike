Why do HVAC companies lose so many calls?

HVAC demand spikes with the weather: the first heatwave or cold snap can multiply call volume while technicians are already on jobs and the office is overloaded. After-hours emergency calls often reach voicemail. Homeowners with no heat or cooling call the next company in the search results rather than waiting.

How does AI triage HVAC emergency calls?

The AI follows the triage rules you set: it asks what is wrong, whether there is no heat or no cooling, any gas smell, water leak or vulnerable occupant, then classifies the call. Emergencies are escalated to the on-call technician with a summary by SMS, and gas or safety issues follow your safety script.

Do TCPA rules apply to AI calls for HVAC companies?

Inbound calls that customers make to you are not telemarketing. Outbound AI calls are different: the FCC ruled in February 2024 that AI-generated voices are "artificial" voices under the TCPA, so marketing calls such as maintenance-plan campaigns need prior express consent. Service calls and texts still need care, so check with counsel.

Source: FCC declaratory ruling FCC 24-17. General information, not legal advice.
