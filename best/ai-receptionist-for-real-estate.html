How did we evaluate AI receptionists for real estate?

We compared real estate AI receptionists and lead-response tools on six points from each vendor's own website: published pricing, real estate CRM integrations, 24/7 coverage, showing or appointment booking, stated claims, and whether each answers inbound calls or mainly follows up leads. We did not place test calls, so use this as a shortlist.

  • Published pricing: the vendor's own pricing page, or "not published".
  • Integrations: which real estate CRMs, such as Follow Up Boss or kvCORE, the vendor names.
  • 24/7 coverage: around the clock, or after hours only.
  • Booking: books on the call, texts a link, or takes a message.
  • Compliance claims: what the vendor states about HIPAA, BAAs or SOC 2. We report claims; we did not audit them.
  • Who it fits: business size, existing software and call volume.

Which real estate AI receptionist fits which agent or team?

The right real estate AI receptionist depends on whether you need inbound calls answered or online leads followed up. Solo agents can start with My AI Front Desk. Teams on Follow Up Boss should compare Smith.ai and Structurely. Teams buying online leads may prefer Ylopo, and brokerages wanting text-first response can look at Verse.

Structurely

Best for: teams and brokerages wanting AI lead qualification tied to a real estate CRM.

Structurely runs inbound and outbound AI voice, SMS and email, always on, with live transfers and scheduling. Its integration logos include Follow Up Boss, BoomTown, Bonzo, HubSpot and Salesforce. Limitation: plan fees need a sales call (usage listed from $0.07 a minute voice with your own carrier), and it is built for lead nurture more than simple call answering.

Ylopo

Best for: teams buying online leads that want AI calling bundled with lead generation.

Ylopo is a lead-generation platform with AI text and AI Voice, which calls leads, qualifies timeline and budget and warm-transfers to agents. AI Voice is included on Growing Team and Complete plans, not Solo. Limitation: AI Voice is described as outbound follow-up, not inbound answering, and pricing is quote-only.

Verse

Best for: brokerages wanting fast AI lead response by text with live transfers and human backup.

Verse runs AI agents for SMS, email, webchat and voice 24/7/365, with an optional managed human concierge, live call transfers and appointment setting. It names Salesforce and HubSpot integrations plus an API. Limitation: it serves many industries, inbound call answering is not stated, and no real estate CRM is named.

Smith.ai

Best for: agents and teams wanting inbound calls answered and logged in a real estate CRM.

Smith.ai names Follow Up Boss, kvCORE and LionDesk integrations, qualifies prospects, schedules showings, covers 24/7 with human escalation and offers bilingual service. Limitation: billing is per call, and human plans cost more per call than AI-only tools.

My AI Front Desk

Best for: solo agents wanting low-cost AI answering and showing booking.

My AI Front Desk answers calls and chats 24/7, books showings, collects budget, timeline, areas and pre-approval status, supports 20+ languages and includes Zapier. Limitation: its real estate page names no real estate CRM, and overage is credit-based.

Ruby

Best for: agents wanting a person to answer calls while they are at showings.

Ruby uses live US-based receptionists 24/7/365, schedules appointments through scheduling platforms and offers Spanish and bilingual handling. Limitation: per-minute billing with no published overage rate, and no real estate CRM is named.

Hyperwrike

Best for: teams and brokerages losing significant commission to slow lead response, missed calls and cold databases.

Hyperwrike builds and runs voice and WhatsApp lead response, qualification, viewing booking, follow-up and weekly reporting. Limitation: it costs $10,000 to $25,000 a month, far more than any tool above.

A cheaper AI receptionist is the better choice for most solo agents and small or low-volume teams. If you mainly miss calls during showings, start with a $50–$300 a month software tool, such as My AI Front Desk at $99 or Smith.ai's AI Pro plan at $150, and track booked showings first.

How should a real estate team set up AI lead response?

A real estate team should set up AI lead response in a fixed order: decide which channels it covers, write the qualifying questions, connect it to the CRM so every conversation is logged, set rules for live transfers to agents, and test with real enquiries before sending paid lead traffic to it.

  1. List lead sources and phone lines, and when each goes unanswered.
  2. Write qualifying questions: buy or rent, area, budget, financing, timeline.
  3. Connect to Follow Up Boss, kvCORE or your CRM and confirm notes are logged.
  4. Set live-transfer and showing-booking rules per agent.
  5. Check consent before any outbound calls or texts.
  6. Review conversations weekly and adjust the script.

Frequently asked questions

How do real estate teams connect AI calling to Follow Up Boss?

Choose a tool that names Follow Up Boss, such as Smith.ai or Structurely, or connect another tool through Zapier or an API. Test that call notes, lead stage and appointments appear in Follow Up Boss.

Can an AI agent answer real estate calls after hours and log them in Follow Up Boss?

Yes, if the tool answers inbound calls and integrates with Follow Up Boss. Smith.ai lists both; Ylopo's AI Voice is described as outbound follow-up, so check inbound support before you buy.

Should a solo agent use an AI receptionist or a live answering service?

A solo agent with modest call volume can start with low-cost AI. Choose live people, such as Ruby, if callers expect a human. See AI vs answering service.

Do I need consent before an AI calls or texts my old leads?

Often yes. Marketing calls and texts, especially with AI voices, carry consent rules in the US, UK and UAE. Check your consent records first; see what an AI voice agent is. General information, not legal advice.
