AI receptionist for UK dental practices

By the Hyperwrike team · Last reviewed 13 November 2026

Book a free 20-minute audit
Short answer: An AI receptionist for a UK dental practice answers every call, including lunchtime and after hours, books new and existing patients into your practice software, sends reminders that reduce failed-to-attend appointments, and runs recall. It must be set up to meet UK GDPR, because dental records are special-category health data.

Why do UK dental practices miss so many calls?

UK dental receptionists juggle patients at the desk, payments and phones at the same time, and call volume peaks at opening time and lunchtime. Calls that ring out often go to the next practice on the list, especially private enquiries for implants, aligners and whitening, where each new patient is worth the most.

What can an AI receptionist do for an NHS or private dental practice?

For mixed NHS and private practices, an AI receptionist can explain availability honestly, add NHS enquiries to a waiting list, book private consultations, handle reschedules and cancellations, send reminders to reduce failed-to-attend appointments, and recall patients due a check-up or hygiene visit. Clinical questions and emergencies go to the team.

Which UK dental software can an AI receptionist work with?

An AI receptionist can book directly only if your practice-management system allows integration. Common UK systems include Dentally, Software of Excellence (SOE) and others with APIs or online-booking links. Where direct integration is not possible, the AI books through your online booking page or sends structured requests to reception.

Check integration options with your software supplier before you buy any AI receptionist. Hyperwrike confirms this during the free audit.

How does UK GDPR apply to a dental AI receptionist?

Under UK GDPR, information about a patient's dental health is special-category data, so the practice needs a lawful basis and an additional condition for processing, clear privacy information, data processing agreements with suppliers, sensible retention periods and security controls. Callers should be told they are speaking with an AI assistant and whether calls are recorded.

Frequently asked questions

Will patients mind talking to an AI receptionist?

Most patients mainly want a quick answer and a booked appointment. Tell them it is an AI assistant, keep a clear option to reach a person, and review call summaries in the first weeks.

Can the AI take card payments or deposits?

It can send a secure payment link by SMS or email if your systems support it. Do not let any AI read card numbers aloud on a call.

Does it work on our existing practice number?

Yes. Calls can be forwarded to the AI when lines are busy, unanswered or closed, so your number does not change.
