Personal injury, immigration, and family law firms lose high-value consultations every single day. Legal emergencies do not happen exclusively between 9 AM and 5 PM.

The after-hours consultation crisis

If a potential client calls at 7 PM and reaches a voicemail, they do not wait — they immediately call the next firm on Google. Law firms average 9 missed consultation requests per week. Depending on your average case value, that translates to tens of thousands of dollars in lost retainer fees monthly.

To fix this, firms are moving away from traditional call centres and deploying specialised AI intake software.

Automating the legal workflow

A bespoke AI legal system does far more than answer the phone. It serves as an intelligent operational layer that handles the three most time-consuming bottlenecks in a law firm.

  1. 24/7 intelligent intake & booking — asks pre-defined qualifying questions to determine case viability, collects details, and books to the correct attorney's calendar with no double-booking.
  2. Automated follow-up sequences — a lead that doesn't convert immediately triggers multi-touch SMS and email follow-up, so no inquiry ever goes cold.
  3. Legal document summarisation AI — instantly summarises 40-page evidence packs, highlights critical clauses, and prepares a briefing before the consultation begins.
