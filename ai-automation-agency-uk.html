Is an AI receptionist compliant with UK GDPR and PECR?

An AI receptionist can be run compliantly in the UK. The main rules are UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 for personal data, call-recording notices for callers, and PECR, which requires specific prior consent before making automated marketing calls.

  • Inbound calls: tell callers they are speaking with an AI assistant and whether the call is recorded, and link to your privacy notice.
  • Outbound marketing: automated marketing calls need prior specific consent under PECR. Service calls such as appointment reminders are treated differently from marketing.
  • Data: keep a record of where call data is processed and stored, set retention periods, and sign a data processing agreement with every supplier.

Guidance: ICO guide to PECR (electronic and telephone marketing), ICO UK GDPR guidance. This page is general information, not legal advice.

Is an AI receptionist legal in the UK?

Yes. Answering inbound calls with AI is legal. You must handle personal data under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, tell callers when calls are recorded, and get specific prior consent before making automated marketing calls under PECR.

Can a UK AI receptionist use a UK phone number?

Yes. The AI can answer on a UK geographic, mobile or 0800 number, either a new number or by forwarding your existing line when it is busy, unanswered or out of hours.
