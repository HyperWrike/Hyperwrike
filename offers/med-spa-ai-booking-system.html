Your Med Spa Is Losing Thousands Every Month to Slow Lead Response

Med spas lose 20-40% of ad-generated leads because staff can't respond fast enough. Hyperwrike's AI agent responds to every inquiry within 60 seconds, books the consult, and reduces no-shows — recovering $3,000-$10,000+ in monthly revenue for a typical single-location med spa.

The Problem

Your front desk is busy performing treatments, not answering Instagram DMs. Every day, dozens of inquiries from your ad spend sit unanswered for hours — sometimes days. Industry research shows that response time is the single biggest factor in whether a lead ever becomes a patient: after just 5 minutes, conversion rates drop by more than 80%. On top of that, no-show rates for aesthetic consults typically run between 20% and 35%, meaning even the leads you do book often never show up.

You're already paying for the ad spend that generates these leads. The problem isn't marketing — it's what happens in the 10 minutes after someone reaches out.

How We Solve It

Hyperwrike builds and manages an AI agent trained specifically on your services, pricing structure, and booking calendar. It lives inside your Instagram DMs, SMS, and website chat, and does three things:

  1. Responds instantly — every inquiry gets a reply within 60 seconds, 24 hours a day, even outside business hours
  2. Qualifies and books — the agent asks the right questions (treatment interest, timing, budget signals) and books directly into your existing calendar system
  3. Reduces no-shows — an automated reminder sequence at 24 hours and 2 hours before each appointment, with an easy reschedule option if someone goes quiet

You don't need to hire another front desk employee or ask your existing team to split their attention between clients in the chair and a phone that won't stop buzzing. The agent handles the volume; your team handles the treatments.

What You Get

  • A fully configured AI agent live within 5-7 business days
  • Integration with your existing booking software (Boulevard, Vagaro, Mindbody, or similar)
  • A no-show reduction sequence running automatically on every booked appointment
  • Monthly performance reporting: leads captured, consults booked, show rate, revenue recovered
  • A dedicated point of contact for adjustments and optimization

Why This Pays for Itself

At an average treatment ticket of $400-$1,200, recovering just 15-20 previously-dead leads per month generates $3,000-$7,000 in new revenue. Add in the no-show recovery — often cutting no-show rates in half — and most clinics see $6,000-$12,000 in recovered monthly revenue against a fraction of that in fees.

Who This Is For

Single or multi-location med spas and aesthetic clinics spending at least $3,000/month on paid advertising, offering injectables, laser treatments, body contouring, or similar high-margin services.
