How did we evaluate AI receptionists for HVAC companies?

We compared HVAC AI receptionists and answering services on six points from each vendor's own website: published pricing, field-service software integrations, 24/7 coverage, booking jobs on the call, emergency handling and stated claims, and the size of company each suits. We did not place test calls, so use this as a shortlist.

  • Published pricing: the vendor's own pricing page, or "not published".
  • Integrations: which field-service management (FSM) platforms, such as ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro, the vendor names.
  • 24/7 coverage: around the clock, or after hours only.
  • Booking: books on the call, texts a link, or takes a message.
  • Compliance claims: what the vendor states about HIPAA, BAAs or SOC 2. We report claims; we did not audit them.
  • Who it fits: business size, existing software and call volume.

Sources are each vendor's own pages, checked 13 November 2026. We did not test call quality. Hyperwrike is our own service, listed as the done-for-you option.

Which HVAC AI receptionist fits which company?

The right HVAC AI receptionist depends mostly on your field-service software. ServiceTitan shops should compare ServiceTitan's own agent, Avoca AI and Sameday AI. Small operators without an FSM can start with Rosie. Companies that want people answering emergency calls should look at PATLive or Smith.ai's human plans.

Avoca AI

Best for: HVAC and home-service companies on ServiceTitan or similar FSM software.

Avoca AI is a ServiceTitan certified app that also shows Housecall Pro and FieldRoutes. Its AI CSR works 24/7, books jobs into the CRM, routes emergencies to technicians and can transfer to Avoca's human CSR team. Limitation: pricing is not published (demo required), and supported languages are not stated.

Sameday AI

Best for: established HVAC shops wanting AI booking connected to field-service software.

Sameday AI integrates with ServiceTitan (certified app), Housecall Pro, FieldRoutes, Service Fusion, JobNimbus and AccuLynx, books jobs directly, speaks English and Spanish, and handles leads from Google Local Services, Angi and Thumbtack. Limitation: the Launch plan starts at $449 a month and covers after hours and weekends only; 24/7 needs Scale. Overage rates are not listed.

ServiceTitan AI Voice Agent

Best for: HVAC companies already running ServiceTitan.

ServiceTitan's AI Voice Agent is built into ServiceTitan, books, confirms and reschedules jobs in real time, transfers to a live CSR and provides transcripts and summaries. Limitation: it requires ServiceTitan, and pricing, languages and 24/7 coverage are not stated on the product page.

Smith.ai

Best for: HVAC businesses wanting AI answering plus a human option from one vendor.

Smith.ai integrates with ServiceTitan, offers AI and live receptionists with emergency dispatch coordination, 24/7 coverage and a bilingual option. Limitation: billing is per call, and human plans ($300 a month for 30 calls) cost more per call than AI-only tools.

Rosie

Best for: small HVAC operators wanting low-cost 24/7 AI answering.

Rosie answers 24/7 on every plan, in English and Spanish, books into a calendar on Scale and above, and texts booking links on the base plan. It connects through Zapier. Limitation: no field-service software is named, it does not dispatch technicians, and overage rates are not stated.

PATLive

Best for: HVAC companies wanting human-answered calls and emergency dispatch.

PATLive uses live receptionists and states it does not use AI to answer calls. 24/7 is included on every plan, Spanish costs nothing extra, and Jobber is among its integrations. Limitation: per-minute billing plus taxes and fees, and few HVAC software integrations are listed.

Hyperwrike

Best for: multi-truck HVAC companies losing significant revenue to missed emergency calls and slow follow-up.

Hyperwrike builds and runs the full system: answering, emergency routing, booking, missed-call text-back and weekly reporting. Limitation: it costs $5,000 to $12,000 a month, far more than any tool above.

When is a cheaper AI receptionist the better choice?

A cheaper AI receptionist is the better choice for most small or low-volume HVAC companies. If you run a few trucks and miss calls mainly after hours, start with a $50–$300 a month software tool, such as Rosie from $49 or Smith.ai Pro at $150, and track the jobs it books before spending more.

How should an HVAC company test an AI receptionist before peak season?

An HVAC company should test an AI receptionist at least a month before peak season. Book a real job into your own field-service software, call it with a no-heat emergency and a gas smell, check who gets alerted and how fast, and model the monthly bill at your busiest week's call volume.

  1. Count missed and after-hours calls in your busiest recent week.
  2. Have the vendor book a test job into your own ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro or Jobber account.
  3. Place test emergency calls and confirm the on-call technician is alerted.
  4. Test Spanish if your callers need it.
  5. Price the plan at peak-week minutes or calls, including overage.
  6. Review transcripts weekly through the first season.

Frequently asked questions

Can an AI receptionist book jobs directly into ServiceTitan?

Yes, several can. ServiceTitan's own voice agent is built in, Avoca AI and Sameday AI are ServiceTitan certified apps, and Smith.ai lists a ServiceTitan integration. Ask for a test booking in your own account.

What features does an AI virtual agent need for home service contractors?

It needs direct booking into your FSM, emergency triage with technician alerts, 24/7 or after-hours cover, Spanish if your area needs it, text confirmations, call summaries and a clear way to reach a person.

What do customers think when an AI receptionist answers a contractor's phone?

We have no survey data to share. In practice, callers mainly want the problem booked quickly. Tell them it is an AI assistant, keep a route to a person, and read transcripts to spot frustration early.

What are the best answering service options for HVAC companies?

For live people, PATLive includes 24/7 and emergency dispatch on every plan, and Smith.ai offers human receptionists alongside AI. See AI vs answering service to choose between them.

What is the best AI virtual receptionist for HVAC companies?

There is no single best option. ServiceTitan shops should compare ServiceTitan's agent, Avoca AI and Sameday AI; small operators can start with Rosie; companies with major missed-call losses can consider a done-for-you build.
