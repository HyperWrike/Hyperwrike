A fully loaded human SDR costs between $110,000 and $139,000 per year — and on average books only 8 to 15 meetings per month. When you pay a human to do the work of a machine, you get human limitations.

The broken B2B sales model

For B2B agencies, SaaS businesses, and consultancies, generating a consistent, qualified pipeline is the hardest part of scaling. The traditional solution is to hire a human Sales Development Representative — but paying a human to do this work is fundamentally inefficient. 80% of an SDR's job is highly repetitive: manually scraping LinkedIn, finding contact details, writing emails, and tracking follow-ups in a CRM.

How an AI SDR outreach system works

An end-to-end AI lead generation infrastructure eliminates the manual bottleneck. Instead of relying on a human to grind through spreadsheets, a custom AI SDR operates continuously in the background.

  1. High-intent prospect identification — scrapes Google Maps, LinkedIn, Apollo, and industry directories to find prospects matching your ICP. Researches over 1,000 accounts per day.
  2. Hyper-personalised outreach — AI writing agents craft and send up to 5,000 personalised emails and LinkedIn messages per week, each tailored to the prospect's business data.
  3. Automated multi-touch follow-up — a relentless sequence across email, LinkedIn, SMS, and voice. The AI never takes a holiday, gets sick, or loses motivation.
  4. Qualification & calendar booking — when a prospect replies, the AI qualifier handles the conversation and places a pre-qualified meeting directly onto your calendar.
