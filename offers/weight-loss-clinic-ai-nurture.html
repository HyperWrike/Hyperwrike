Stop Paying for Leads Your Clinic Never Converts

Medical weight-loss and GLP-1 clinics typically convert less than 30% of paid leads because the decision cycle takes 1-3 weeks and most clinics only follow up once. HyperWrike's AI nurture agent runs a structured multi-touch sequence that recovers 5-8 additional patients per month.

The Problem

Weight-loss leads are expensive — often $40 to $80 each — and the decision to start a program isn't made in one phone call. Patients need time to weigh cost, side effects, and commitment. Most clinics call once or twice, then move on to the next lead, leaving 70% or more of paid leads to go completely cold.

How We Solve It

We build a multi-touch AI nurture sequence across SMS and email that stays with each lead for the full length of their decision window — typically 21 days, with a minimum of 7 touchpoints. The agent is trained to handle the specific objections that stall weight-loss decisions: cost concerns, safety questions, insurance confusion, and simple procrastination. Anyone who books a consult and doesn't show gets an automatic re-engagement sequence rather than falling off entirely.

What You Get

  • A full nurture sequence built around your specific program and pricing
  • AI SMS/email agent that responds to inbound replies in real time
  • Direct booking integration with your calendar
  • Weekly lead-status reporting so you always know where every lead stands

Why This Pays for Itself

At a program value of $2,000-$5,000, converting just 5-8 additional patients per month from leads that would otherwise be abandoned generates $12,500-$20,000 in recovered monthly revenue.

Who This Is For

Medical weight-loss clinics and GLP-1 telehealth providers spending at least $5,000/month on paid lead generation.
