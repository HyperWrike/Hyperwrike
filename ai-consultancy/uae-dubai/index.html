Why Do UAE Systems Start With WhatsApp?

In the UAE, most customers begin a conversation on WhatsApp rather than by email or web form, and they expect a fast reply, including in the evening and at weekends. A buyer often messages several brokers about the same listing, and the first useful reply usually wins the viewing. So our UAE systems reply on the official WhatsApp Business Platform first, with phone and web as further channels, and hand hot leads to your team with the full conversation attached.

Which Data and Messaging Rules Apply in the UAE?

Customer data falls under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021), or the separate DIFC or ADGM data protection rules for firms registered in those free zones. Outbound marketing calls follow the UAE telemarketing rules, and WhatsApp messages follow WhatsApp's business messaging policy.
  • Data protection: we map what personal data the AI collects, where it is stored and who can see it during discovery, under the PDPL or your free zone's rules.
  • Health data: clinics have extra federal rules on health information, including where it can be stored. We confirm hosting and data handling with you before building a clinic system.
  • Telemarketing: the UAE's telemarketing rules set calling hours and require calls from registered business numbers. Our systems lead with inbound replies and opted-in WhatsApp follow-up. Check current rules with your telecom provider before any outbound campaign.
  • WhatsApp: outbound messages use approved templates and only go to people who have opted in.

References: UAE Government portal: data protection laws · WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy. General information, not legal advice.
