You're Losing $8,000-$25,000 Jobs Because Nobody Follows Up

More than 60% of roofing quotes never receive a follow-up call because sales reps move on to the next estimate. HyperWrike's AI agent automatically follows up on every open quote via text and call until it's closed-won or closed-lost, typically recovering 2-4 additional jobs per month.

The Problem

Your reps are excellent at getting on roofs and quoting jobs — but the moment that quote is sent, they're on to the next appointment. The homeowner is left waiting, gets quotes from two other companies, and goes with whoever calls back first. It's rarely about price. It's about who follows up.

How We Solve It

HyperWrike installs an AI agent that automatically triggers follow-up the moment a quote is logged — via text and call, on a set cadence (day 1, 3, 7, and 14). It's trained to handle the exact objections that stall roofing decisions: "still deciding," "waiting on other quotes," "dealing with insurance." When a lead shows real buying intent, it's instantly escalated to a live rep to close.

What You Get

  • Integration with your existing CRM or quoting software (JobNimbus, AccuLynx, or manual intake)
  • Automated follow-up cadence running on every open quote
  • Real-time hot-lead alerts to your sales team
  • Monthly close-rate reporting

Why This Pays for Itself

At an average ticket of $8,000-$25,000, closing just one additional job per month covers the entire cost of this system. Most clients see 2-4 recovered jobs per month once the system is fully warmed up.

Who This Is For

Roofing and storm restoration companies doing $500,000+ in annual revenue with field-based sales reps.
