## Common Objections & Responses

### "How is this different from [competitor]?"
> "We're not a SaaS tool you have to learn and manage. We build and operate the entire system for you, integrated with your existing CRM. Your team doesn't change anything — they just get more qualified appointments."

### "What if the AI screws up?"
> "Every hot lead gets human review before it goes to your calendar. The AI handles qualification and scheduling — your team still closes. Plus we run a 7-day pilot on your dead leads before you pay a dime."

### "We already have [CRM/tool]."
> "Perfect. We layer on top of what you already use — no rip-and-replace. Your team keeps using [CRM] exactly as they do now; the AI just feeds them more qualified opportunities."

### "Our industry is too complex for AI."
> "That's why we don't use generic chatbots. We build custom agents trained on your specific services, pricing, and objections. The 7-day pilot proves it before you commit."

### "What's the catch?"
> "No catch. We only make money if you keep us — there's no long-term contract. Most clients see ROI in the first 30 days and never want to turn it off."
