What Is the Revenue Impact of Missed Calls for a Dental Practice?

Each missed call to a private dental practice costs an average of $3,247 in lost patient lifetime value. A practice taking 30 calls per day and missing 10 — the industry average of 1 in 3 — loses $32,470 per day in potential lifetime value, equivalent to over $600,000 in annual LTV exposure.

The per-call loss is larger than it appears because dental patient relationships are long-term. A patient who books a new patient consultation becomes a check-up patient, a hygiene patient, and eventually a treatment patient. Missing the initial call doesn't lose one appointment — it loses the entire relationship.

  • Roughly 1 in 4 calls to dental practices goes unanswered (27%, Invoca call data, 2023). (source)
  • Each missed new-patient call can cost a practice thousands in lifetime value (your number depends on case mix and retention).

How Does an AI Receptionist Work for a Dental Practice?

An AI dental receptionist answers every inbound call within two rings, identifies whether the caller is a new or existing patient, answers treatment and pricing questions from the practice's knowledge base, and books appointments directly into the practice management system — 24 hours a day, including evenings and weekends.

The AI integrates with major dental practice management systems including Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Carestream, and Exact to access real-time availability. Emergency dental enquiries — toothache, broken crown, lost filling — are triaged by urgency and routed to emergency slots or on-call staff with a full call summary.

What Is Dental Patient Recall Automation?

Dental patient recall automation is the use of AI to systematically contact every patient due for a 6-month check-up or hygiene appointment, deliver a personalised message referencing their last visit and the treatment due, and book the appointment directly — without front desk involvement.

A practice with 2,000 active patients generates 330 recall appointments per month when running systematic recall. AI automation ensures every patient in the recall window is contacted, not just those the front desk remembers to call.

How Does Dental No-Show Prevention Work?

Dental no-show prevention AI sends four automated reminders before each appointment — at 7 days, 48 hours, 24 hours, and 2 hours before — across SMS, voice call, and WhatsApp. Automated reminders cut no-shows by about 23% across 64 dental practices (Sesame Communications data, 2013). (source)

When a patient reschedules through the reminder sequence, the AI immediately offers the released slot to patients on the waitlist — The net effect is higher chair utilisation without any increase in front desk workload.
