What is speed-to-lead?

By the Hyperwrike team · Last reviewed 13 November 2026

Book a free 20-minute audit
Short answer: Speed-to-lead is the time between a prospect enquiring (a form, call, chat or ad lead) and a business making real contact with them. Shorter is better: research on lead response has repeatedly found that responding within minutes, rather than hours, makes contact and conversion far more likely.

Why does speed-to-lead matter?

Speed-to-lead matters because new enquiries cool quickly and often contact several businesses at once. The first business to respond usually gets the conversation. The widely cited Harvard Business Review lead-response study found firms that responded within an hour were far more likely to qualify a lead than those that waited longer.

Source: Harvard Business Review, The Short Life of Online Sales Leads (2011). See more figures in our speed-to-lead benchmarks.

How do you measure speed-to-lead?

Measure speed-to-lead as the median time from enquiry timestamp to first real two-way contact, not to an automatic acknowledgement email. Track it by channel (web form, phone, ads, WhatsApp) and by hour of day, because evenings and weekends are usually where response times collapse.

  1. Export enquiry timestamps from your CRM, forms and ad platforms.
  2. Record the time of the first real conversation (call connected, reply received).
  3. Report the median and the share answered within 5 minutes and within 1 hour.
  4. Split by channel and by business hours vs after hours.

How does AI improve speed-to-lead?

AI improves speed-to-lead by responding to every new enquiry within seconds, at any hour: calling the lead back, sending an SMS or WhatsApp message, asking qualifying questions and booking a call or appointment. Your team then speaks to leads who are already qualified and booked, instead of chasing cold ones the next morning.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good speed-to-lead time?

Aim for first contact within five minutes during business hours, and an automatic response within a minute at all times. AI makes the one-minute target realistic 24/7.

Does an auto-reply email count as a response?

Not really. Track the time to the first real two-way conversation; auto-replies help but rarely convert on their own.

Which industries depend most on speed-to-lead?

Industries where buyers enquire with several providers at once: mortgage, real estate, home services, aesthetics, legal and high-ticket coaching.

How do I start improving speed-to-lead?

Measure your current median response time, then add instant automated callbacks or messages for every new enquiry and route qualified leads to your team.
