Why do weight-loss clinic leads go cold?

Weight-loss clinic leads go cold because starting a medical weight-loss or GLP-1 programme is a considered decision. People weigh cost, safety questions, insurance and commitment over days or weeks, while many clinics call once or twice and move on. The clinic that keeps helpfully in touch through that decision usually gets the consultation.

The fix is follow-up that lasts as long as the decision does, answers the practical questions quickly and makes booking easy. Read what speed-to-lead means and see sourced response-time figures in our speed-to-lead benchmarks.

How does an AI nurture sequence work for a weight-loss clinic?

An AI nurture sequence replies to each new lead within a minute, asks a few non-clinical questions, offers consultation times, and then follows up by SMS, email or WhatsApp across the weeks people take to decide. It answers replies in real time, books directly into your calendar and re-engages anyone who misses a consultation.

  1. Instant reply to every ad, form or call enquiry, day or night.
  2. Short qualification: goals, preferred location or telehealth, timing and budget questions you approve.
  3. Consultation offer with live calendar slots.
  4. Follow-up touches over the decision window you set, each answering a common question about cost, process or next steps.
  5. Clinical questions routed to your clinical team, never answered by the AI.
  6. Automatic re-engagement for missed consultations, and weekly lead-status reporting.

What can the AI answer, and what must go to a clinician?

The AI answers practical questions only: programme structure, consultation process, prices you publish, payment options, location, opening hours and how to book. Anything about eligibility, medication, dosing, side effects, pregnancy or existing conditions goes to a clinician. The AI never gives clinical advice, and your clinical lead approves every script before launch.

What compliance rules apply to AI follow-up for GLP-1 clinics?

Several sets of rules can apply. In the US, a vendor that handles patient information for a clinic is usually a HIPAA business associate, and automated marketing calls and texts need consent under the TCPA. In the UK, health data is special-category data under UK GDPR. Check your set-up and claims with counsel and compliance.

Official guidance: HHS guidance on HIPAA business associates; FCC declaratory ruling on AI-generated voices (FCC 24-17); ICO UK GDPR guidance. Advertising rules for prescription medicines and health claims also apply in both markets. General information, not legal advice. Check with your own counsel or compliance adviser. Hyperwrike does not claim HIPAA or other certifications.
