How does the missed-call calculator work?

The missed-call calculator multiplies four numbers: missed calls per week, 4.33 weeks per month, the share of callers who would have booked, and the average value of a booking. The result is the revenue you are likely losing each month. It is an estimate, so use your own call logs and booking data.

  1. Missed calls per week: take one normal week from your phone system's call log (unanswered + voicemail + abandoned).
  2. Booking rate: use your normal conversion from answered enquiries to bookings.
  3. Value per booking: use the first visit or first job value, not lifetime value, to stay conservative.

Frequently asked questions

Is the missed-call calculator free?

Yes. It is free, needs no sign-up, and runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to Hyperwrike.

Where do I find my missed-call numbers?

Most phone systems and VoIP providers show unanswered, abandoned and voicemail calls in their call logs. Take one typical week and use that figure.

Why does the calculator use 4.33 weeks?

A year has 52 weeks, and 52 divided by 12 is about 4.33, so weekly figures multiplied by 4.33 give a monthly estimate.

Should I use first-visit value or lifetime value?

Use first-visit or first-job value for a conservative estimate. Lifetime value shows the bigger picture but can overstate what a single missed call costs.
