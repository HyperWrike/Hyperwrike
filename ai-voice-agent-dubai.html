What rules apply to AI calling and messaging in the UAE?

UAE businesses using AI agents should follow the UAE Personal Data Protection Law for customer data, the separate data-protection rules in DIFC or ADGM if they are registered there, WhatsApp's business messaging policies, and the UAE telemarketing regulation for outbound marketing calls.

  • Data protection: Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (PDPL); DIFC and ADGM have their own data-protection laws.
  • Telemarketing: the UAE's telemarketing rules set calling hours and require calls from registered business numbers. Check the current rules with your telecom provider before running outbound campaigns.
  • WhatsApp: use the official WhatsApp Business Platform with approved templates and opt-ins for outbound messages.

This page is general information, not legal advice. Reference: UAE Government portal: data protection laws.
