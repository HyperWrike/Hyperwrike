How Much Does an AI Consultancy Cost per Month?

Published 2026 guides put advisory-only AI consulting retainers at roughly $2,000–$8,000 a month and hands-on managed work at roughly $5,000–$25,000 a month. Hyperwrike's retainers, which include building and running the system, range from $5K to $40K a month depending on the industry and scope.

What Is Included in the Retainer?

  • Discovery, architecture, build, deployment and training. This takes about 21 days from the first call to a live system.
  • Integration with the phone system, CRM, calendar, WhatsApp and document tools you already use.
  • Monitoring and optimisation of every conversation after launch.
  • A weekly report on hours saved, calls handled and revenue recovered.
  • Ownership. You own the system and its documentation.
