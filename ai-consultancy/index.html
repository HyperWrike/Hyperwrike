What Does an AI Consultancy Do?

An AI consultancy finds where AI can save a business money or win it more revenue, designs the system, and helps put it into production. Hyperwrike does all three and then runs the system: it finds the revenue leak, builds the AI system that closes it in about 21 days, and monitors and improves it on a monthly retainer.

Most AI projects stall between the strategy deck and production. We start from a single measurable leak, such as calls that go to voicemail, leads called back hours late or documents re-typed by hand, and put a number on what it costs you each month. Then we build the system that fixes that one leak, connect it to the tools you already use, and report on it every week. Once it pays for itself, we move to the next leak.

What Does an Engagement Look Like?

  1. Free 20-minute audit. We look at one workflow and put a monthly number on the leak. No pitch deck.
  2. Discovery and architecture. We map your call flow, CRM, documents and rules, and agree what the system will and won't do.
  3. Build and integrate. We build the AI voice, WhatsApp, document or CRM system and connect it to the tools you already use.
  4. Deploy and train. The system goes live and your team learns how to work with it. This is about 21 days from the first call.
  5. Run and improve. On a monthly retainer we monitor every conversation, fix what goes wrong and report each week on hours saved, calls handled and revenue recovered.

Results from live systems in India include VS Hospitals in Chennai (40% fewer no-shows and zero missed after-hours calls) and Panasuna Chit Funds (100+ hours of manual work saved each month).

Where Does Hyperwrike Work?

We are based in Chennai, India and build for businesses in these markets. Each one has its own rules for AI calls, messages and data, and we design for them from the start. This is a summary, not legal advice.

United States, including New York

Outbound AI voice calls fall under the TCPA: in 2024 the FCC confirmed that AI-generated voices count as "artificial" voices, so they need the caller's prior consent. In New York City, automated tools used in hiring decisions need a bias audit under Local Law 144. Inbound answering, which covers most of our HVAC, roofing, dental and legal systems, is the simpler case. See AI consultancy for US businesses.

United Kingdom

Systems are designed around UK GDPR and PECR, which governs marketing calls and texts. See AI consultancy for UK businesses.

UAE and Dubai

Data handling follows the UAE's federal data protection law (PDPL), or the DIFC and ADGM rules for firms in those free zones. Most buyers expect WhatsApp first, and agents can work in English with Arabic scoped per project. See AI consultancy for Dubai and the UAE.

Kuwait and the GCC

As in the UAE, enquiries arrive mostly on WhatsApp and in two languages. We scope Arabic and English agents for real estate, clinics and retail groups, and design data handling around Kuwait's data privacy rules. See AI consultancy for Kuwait.

Netherlands and the EU

Systems follow GDPR. Under the EU AI Act, people must be told when they are talking to an AI system, and these transparency rules came into effect in August 2026. Our voice and chat agents disclose that they are AI by default. See AI consultancy for the Netherlands.

Canada

Systems follow PIPEDA and, for Quebec, Law 25. Commercial texts and emails must comply with CASL. See AI consultancy for Canadian businesses.
