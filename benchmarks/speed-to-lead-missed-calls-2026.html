Speed-to-lead & missed-call benchmarks 2026

By the Hyperwrike team · Last reviewed 13 November 2026

Book a free 20-minute audit
Short answer: Businesses lose leads to slow replies and unanswered calls. Leads called within 5 minutes were about 21 times likelier to qualify than those called after 30 minutes, about 27% of calls to dental and home-services firms are missed, and only 15% of missed callers leave a voicemail. All figures below are sourced.

How much does lead response time affect conversion?

Lead response time has a large effect on conversion. The MIT/InsideSales Lead Response Management study found web leads called within 5 minutes were about 21 times more likely to be qualified than leads called after 30 minutes. Harvard Business Review research found only 37% of companies responded within an hour.

How many business calls go unanswered?

A large share of business calls go unanswered. Invoca's call data shows about 27% of calls to dental and home-services businesses are missed, and 49% to banks and lenders. Clio's 2024 secret-shopper study found 48% of law firms did not answer or return a call. Only about half of home-services callers reached a person.

Do callers leave voicemails, and do UK businesses answer?

Most callers do not leave voicemails. Quo's 2026 platform data shows only 15% of missed callers left a voicemail, and 69% got no callback within 48 hours. In the UK, a Moneypenny survey found a third of micro businesses failed to answer calls, and BT research found customers try a business twice at most.

What are typical patient no-show rates, and do reminders help?

No-show rates vary widely by setting. A 2018 systematic review of 105 studies put the average outpatient no-show rate near 23%. A Cochrane review found SMS reminders raised attendance from 67.8% to 78.6%. US dental benchmarks from practice-software vendors report much lower dental no-show rates, around 3%.

How fast do real estate and mortgage firms respond to leads?

Real estate and mortgage firms are often slow to respond. Pied Piper's 2026 mystery-shop study found only 10% of web inquiries to large US brokerages got agent contact within an hour, and only a third of calls reached an agent. An earlier Insellerate test found just 13% of mortgage companies responded within an hour.

What do customers expect, and how do UAE customers prefer to message?

Customers expect fast replies. HubSpot research found 90% rate an immediate response as important for service questions, and 60% define immediate as 10 minutes or less. In the UAE, WhatsApp is used monthly by about 86% of internet users aged 16 to 64, so WhatsApp-first response matters for UAE businesses.

How was this benchmark compiled?

This page is a compilation of published third-party statistics, not original Hyperwrike research. Each figure was checked against its source on 13 November 2026. Primary sources are the original study or dataset; secondary reports quote a study we could not open directly. Vendor platform data is labelled, because it reflects each vendor's customer base.

  • Inclusion: a figure is listed only if its source page could be opened and the number matched.
  • Excluded: widely repeated numbers whose original source could not be traced (for example "50% of leads never get called back").
  • Limitations: studies differ in year, sample, industry and definition of "missed" or "response", so compare figures with care.
  • Updates: reviewed quarterly; the last-reviewed date is shown at the top of the page.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good speed-to-lead time?

Aim to make real contact within five minutes. The MIT/InsideSales study found the odds of qualifying a lead dropped sharply after the first five minutes, and HBR research found most companies took far longer than an hour.

What share of business calls are missed?

It depends on the industry. Invoca's data shows about 27% of calls to dental and home-services businesses are missed and 49% to banks and lenders; a 2024 Clio secret-shopper study found 48% of law firms did not answer or return a call.

Do SMS reminders reduce no-shows?

Yes. A Cochrane review of randomised trials found text-message reminders raised appointment attendance from 67.8% to 78.6% compared with no reminder.
