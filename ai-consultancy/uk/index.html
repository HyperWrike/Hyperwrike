Which UK Rules Apply to AI Calls and Messages?

Answering inbound calls with AI is legal in the UK. Personal data must be handled under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, callers should be told they are speaking with an AI assistant and whether the call is recorded, and automated marketing calls need specific prior consent under PECR.
  • Marketing calls and messages (PECR): automated marketing calls need specific prior consent. Marketing texts and emails to individuals need consent, apart from the "soft opt-in" for existing customers. Live marketing calls must be screened against the TPS and CTPS. Service messages, such as appointment reminders, are treated differently from marketing.
  • Health data: information about a patient's health is special-category data under UK GDPR, so clinics need an extra condition for processing, clear privacy information and data processing agreements with every supplier.
  • Mortgage brokers: firms regulated by the FCA must meet the Consumer Duty, so AI follow-up has to give clear, fair information and an easy route to a human adviser.
  • Records: we document where call data is processed and stored, set retention periods, and give you the paperwork for your own records.

Guidance: ICO guide to PECR · ICO UK GDPR guidance · FCA Consumer Duty. General information, not legal advice.
